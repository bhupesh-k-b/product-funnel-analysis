# Findings

Analyzed 2,755,641 distinct observed events from 1,407,580 visitors.

## Ordered funnel

- Product view: 1,345,063 visitors (100.00% of eligible viewers).
- Add to cart: 28,813 visitors (2.14% of eligible viewers).
- Purchase: 9,052 visitors (0.67% of eligible viewers).

## Three behavioral signals

Features are measured in the first 24 hours, before the purchase outcome in days 2–7. Early purchasers are excluded. These are associations, not causal drivers.

- First-day cart activity, Cart added: 0.912% later purchase rate (n=23,568).
- First-day cart activity, No cart: 0.055% later purchase rate (n=1,315,477).
- First-day product breadth, 0-1 products: 0.042% later purchase rate (n=1,137,032).
- First-day product breadth, 2+ products: 0.227% later purchase rate (n=202,013).
- First-day product views, 0-1 views: 0.032% later purchase rate (n=1,040,158).
- First-day product views, 2+ views: 0.203% later purchase rate (n=298,887).

## Product priorities

1. Investigate product-detail clarity and add-to-cart friction at the largest absolute drop-off.
2. Test cart recovery and onboarding reminders in a randomized experiment; use purchase conversion and opt-out rate as success/guardrail metrics.
3. Validate the above signals in a time-separated cohort before using them for targeting.

No onboarding intervention or A/B assignment exists in this source. It cannot establish an 18% conversion lift or a 9% retention improvement. Revenue amounts and referral events are unavailable.
